# Research record: festivals, sacred places and family companions

Checked online 2026-10-10. This is an implementation research record, not public page copy. New adaptations are original summaries, not reproduced translations. No contributed photograph was assigned an unverified temple identity. No date calculator, travel itinerary or festival schedule was added.

## Evidence and editorial boundaries

| Page / claim | Source and extent |
|---|---|
| Maha Shivaratri worship, abhisheka, night vigil, union/dance/Halahala associations, Chidambaram Natyanjali | Government of India Ministry of Tourism, [Maha Shivratri](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/maha-shivratri). Festival associations are labelled devotional explanations, not combined into a single narrative. Promotional health/planetary-energy claims in this source were excluded. |
| Navaratri nine nights; Gujarat garba, Bengal Durga Puja, Tamil Nadu golu | Ministry of Tourism, [Navratri](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/navratri) and [October 2023 newsletter](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-12/Newsletter_Oct.pdf). Examples are not universal prescriptions. |
| Hanuman Jayanti birth celebration and regional calendar differences | Sri Venkateswara Temple, [Sri Hanumajjayanti event flyer](https://www.venkateswaratemple.org/flyer/SriHanumajjayanti.pdf). Search-indexed PDF text distinguishes Tamil Nadu/Kerala and Andhra Pradesh/Telangana traditions; direct browser retrieval encountered a challenge. Page deliberately omits specific dates, tithis and universal scheduling claims. |
| Sundara Kanda recitation in Hanuman Jayanti celebrations | Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams official [2024 press release](https://news.tirumala.org/%E0%B0%9C%E0%B1%82%E0%B0%A8%E0%B1%8D-2%E0%B0%A8-%E0%B0%A7%E0%B0%B0%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%AE%E0%B0%97%E0%B0%BF%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%BF%E0%B0%B2%E0%B1%8B-%E0%B0%B8%E0%B0%82%E0%B0%AA%E0%B1%82/) and [official May 2024 archive](https://news.tirumala.org/2024/05/page/3/). Used as a documented example of practice, not an upcoming event. |
| Varanasi alternate names and geography | Ministry of Tourism, [Varanasi](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttar-pradesh/varanasi); Ministry of Tourism [pilgrimage guidelines](https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-12/Scheme%20Guidelines_8_0.pdf). |
| Kashi Vishwanath as Shiva/jyotirlinga shrine | [Kashi official portal](https://darshan.kashi.gov.in/explore-service/shri-kashi-vishwanath-temple). |
| Rameswaram island and Ramanathaswamy sacred tradition/architecture | Ramanathapuram district government, [Ramanathaswamy Temple](https://ramanathapuram.nic.in/tourist-place/ramanathaswamy-temple/) and [Dhanushkodi](https://ramanathapuram.nic.in/tourist-place/dhanushkodi/). Rama’s Shiva worship is explicitly presented as local sacred tradition. No claim that it belongs to Sundara Kanda sarga 1. |
| Family Shiva adaptation | Bhagavata Purana 8.7, [Bhaktivedanta Book Trust edition](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/7/), especially 1, 7–10, 18–20, 36–43. Keeps Bhavani’s consent and blue mark; does not introduce throat-squeezing from other tellings. |
| Family Hanuman adaptation | Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda sarga 1, [Sanskrit/English edition](https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/sundara/sarga1/sundararoman1.htm). Mainaka, Surasa’s test, Simhika and arrival only. No extended Lanka search or return claims. |

Discussion prompts and narrative connections are editorial reflections. New artwork is an AI-generated interpretation, labelled accordingly. Independent fluent/cultural review has not been claimed.
