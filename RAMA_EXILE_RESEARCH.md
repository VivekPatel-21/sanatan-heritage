## Narrative sequence

- Ayodhya Kanda 2: The proposed royal succession and the assembly’s approval.
- Ayodhya Kanda 9: Manthara’s counsel and the remembered boons.
- Ayodhya Kanda 18: Kaikeyi tells Rama the terms: Bharata’s consecration and fourteen years in the forest.
- Ayodhya Kanda 19: Rama accepts and asks that Bharata be summoned.
- Ayodhya Kanda 30: Sita’s insistence and Rama’s agreement.
- Ayodhya Kanda 31: Lakshmana’s request to accompany them.
- Ayodhya Kanda 40: Family farewells, the chariot and the grieving citizens.
- Ayodhya Kanda 46: The first night at the Tamasa and departure while the citizens sleep.
- Ayodhya Kanda 52: Sumantra’s return, matted hair, the Ganga crossing and Sita’s prayers.
- Ayodhya Kanda 54: Bharadwaja’s hospitality and recommendation of Chitrakuta.
- Ayodhya Kanda 56: The forest landscape, dwelling and entry rites.

## Editorial decisions

The eight chapters cover the beginning of the fourteen-year exile, ending at the first dwelling at Chitrakuta. They do not imply that Rama remains there for all fourteen years. Later Bharata episodes and the return to Ayodhya are outside the reader. Sita’s and Lakshmana’s decisions remain explicit; Bharata is not treated as the exile’s instigator. Extended dialogues, prayers, ritual and descriptions are condensed into original English prose. No long translated quotations are reproduced. The modern reflection is labeled editorial, and later devotional accounts are not merged into this sequence.
