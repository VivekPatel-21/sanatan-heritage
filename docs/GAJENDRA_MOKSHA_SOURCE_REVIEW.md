# Gajendra Moksha source review

Primary narrative: *Srimad Bhagavata Purana*, Skandha 8, chapters 2–4, checked against the owner-supplied Gita Press Sanskrit–Hindi first volume and the Sanskrit/English chapter sequence at VedaBase.

- 8.2.1–19: Trikuta, Ritumata and the lake.
- 8.2.20–26: Gajendra and the herd enter the water.
- 8.2.27–33: crocodile attack, prolonged struggle, helplessness and turn toward the Supreme refuge.
- 8.3.1–29: remembered prayer from Indradyumna’s former life; theological content condensed, not translated.
- 8.3.30–33: Narayana arrives on Garuda, accepts the lotus offering and releases Gajendra.
- 8.4.1–13: Huhu and Indradyumna previous-life accounts; Gajendra’s liberation.
- 8.4.14–26: concluding blessings and recitation material, acknowledged in notes but not turned into prescriptive guidance.

Editorial controls: no modern geographical identification for Trikuta; no conversion of the Puranic thousand years into ordinary chronology; no blending with later regional variants; non-graphic crocodile imagery; rescue and moksha kept conceptually distinct.
