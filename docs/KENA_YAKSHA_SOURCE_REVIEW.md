## Editorial boundaries

- The website text is an original condensed retelling, not a translation.
- The Yaksha’s identity is not disclosed to the reader before Uma reveals it in the narrative.
- Uma Haimavati is treated as the revealer of knowledge, not as a decorative witness.
- Brahman is not confused with Brahma.
- The hero image is disclosed as a composite even though the text presents the three deva encounters in sequence.
- The reflection question and explanatory transitions are editorial.
