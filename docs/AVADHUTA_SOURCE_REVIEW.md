# The Twenty-Four Teachers of the Avadhuta: source review

Owner-supplied edition: Gita Press, *Srimad Bhagavat Mahapuran*, Volume 2, Sanskrit text with Hindi explanation, in the [supplied two-volume set](https://archive.org/details/srimad-bhagavat-mahapuran-2-volume-set-sanskrit-hindi). The local PDF and OCR were inspected for this page and remain ignored research files; neither is published.

The relevant passage is Bhagavata Purana 11.7.19–11.9.25. Volume 2 reflowed PDF positions are approximately pp. 1101–1130; chapter and verse are the reliable references.

- 11.7.19–36: Krishna recounts King Yadu's question to a wandering Avadhuta. Verses 33–35 list the twenty-four teachers. The Sanskrit in this exchange does not name Dattatreya; the edition's Hindi explanation identifies the Avadhuta with him.
- 11.7.37–74: earth, air, sky, water, fire, moon, sun, pigeon.
- 11.8.1–44: python, ocean, moth, bee, elephant, honey gatherer, deer, fish, Pingala.
- 11.9.1–23: kurara bird, child, maiden, arrow maker, snake, spider, wasp and captive insect.
- 11.9.24–25: the body is considered after the completed numbered list, so it is described separately.

The English entry summaries are original prose, not translations. The animal examples, especially the elephant and deer, are kept in the renunciant context of the text. Pingala's change of mind is represented as her own action. Spider and wasp imagery is identified as analogy, not natural science. The illustrations are conceptual and do not claim to portray named or historical figures.
