# Lalita Tripurasundari expansion source review

## Verified supplied source

*Srividyarnavatantram* of Sri Vidyaranyayati, Sanskrit text with Hindi commentary by Sri Kapildev Narayan, Chaukhamba Surbharati Prakashan, five supplied PDF volumes.

- Volume 1 title page: Purvardha, Part One, shvasas 1-11.
- Volume 5 title page: Purvardha, Part Two, shvasas 12-18.
- Volume 4 title page: Uttarardha, Part One, shvasas 19-24.
- Volume 3 title page: Uttarardha, Part Two, shvasas 25-30.
- Volume 2 title page: Uttarardha, Part Three, shvasas 31-36.

## Passages used

Volume 1, PDF pages 6-10, especially the English editorial introduction on printed pages iii-v:

- Mahatripurasundari as the highest reality approached through the feminine divine.
- The editor's explanation of the name as sovereign of three spheres associated with Brahma, Vishnu and Rudra.
- The threefold approach: sthula (gross or bodily form), sukshma (subtle or verbal form) and para (transcendent form as universal consciousness).
- Sri Chakra, divine forms, planets, constellations, Yoginis, zodiacal signs, Shaktis, Nityas and teacher-disciple transmission as coordinated fields within the work.
- Two divisions of eighteen chapters each; the editor's outline of their broad emphases.

## Editorial controls

- Attribute the name explanation and structural overview to the book's introduction rather than presenting them as universally agreed etymology.
- Treat sthula, sukshma and para as the source's threefold framework, not as three separate goddesses.
- Explain relationships without reproducing initiatory mantra, ritual recipes or long translated passages.
- Preserve the existing distinction between the Lalita Mahatmya narrative, Bhavanopanishad commentary and Srividyarnavatantram.
- Keep lineage variation explicit.
