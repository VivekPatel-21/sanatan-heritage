# Infinite Shiva Linga source review

## Edition and scope

The reader uses the J. L. Shastri English translation of the Shiva Purana already linked in the owner-approved source library: Vidyeshvara Samhita 5.25–30 and chapters 6–9. It presents this edition’s Shaiva account rather than combining the separate Rudra Samhita version or later summaries.

## Narrative boundaries

- Preserve the escalation from dispute to world-threatening weapons.
- Preserve the column’s intervention before the searches.
- Keep Vishnu’s boar search and honest return distinct from Brahma’s hamsa search and Ketaki claim.
- Keep Shiva’s response nuanced: truth is honoured, falsehood has consequences, Vishnu intercedes, Brahma retains a sacrificial role, and Ketaki receives a restricted place.
- Present chapter 9’s manifest/unmanifest teaching as the source’s theology, not as a universal ranking slogan.
- Do not silently identify the episode with every Jyotirlinga tradition.
- Keep threatened violence non-graphic.

## Art review

Three unique images were commissioned with built-in Imagegen. The hero shows the limitless central column with Brahma on a hamsa and Vishnu on Garuda; an unintended peacock feather was removed from Vishnu’s crown. The inline image shows Shiva with Uma and was corrected so Vishnu has exactly four arms. The card is a symbolic still life.
