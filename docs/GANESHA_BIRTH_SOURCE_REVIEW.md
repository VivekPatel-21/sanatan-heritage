# The Birth of Ganesha source review

## Supplied edition

- `Shiva Mahapuran Part 1 First Half by Veda Vyas with Explanation and Illustration - Gita Press_text.pdf`: Gita Press Sanskrit–Hindi Shiva Mahapurana, Rudra Samhita, Kumara Khanda, chapters 13–18. Relevant scan pages 798–824; printed pages 788–814.

The second supplied volume contains later samhitas and was not needed for this Kumara Khanda story. Neither PDF is copied into the public build or GitHub.

## Editorial boundaries

- The page follows the supplied sequence: Parvati’s wish for her own attendant; creation and appointment of her son; doorway conflict; beheading; Parvati’s powers and conditions; the northern search and elephant head; restoration; appointment as Ganapati and first-worshipped remover of obstacles.
- The child is human-headed before restoration. No elephant-headed image is used for the creation or doorway chapters.
- Combat catalogues and graphic details are condensed. The beheading is stated but not illustrated.
- Detailed vrata instructions in chapter 18 are acknowledged in the sources rather than converted into modern ritual guidance.
- Other birth traditions are named only as distinct tellings and are not merged into the narrative.
