# Shiva and Andhakasura · source review

## Approved source

J. L. Shastri’s English translation of the *Shiva Purana*, supplied by the site owner and available chapter by chapter through Wisdom Library. This reader follows Rudra Samhita, Yuddha Khanda, chapters 42 and 44–49.

## Narrative boundary

- Chapter 42: Andhaka’s unusual birth, Parvati’s care and adoption by Hiranyaksha.
- Chapter 44: mockery, penance, restored sight, boon, conquest and the demand concerning Parvati.
- Chapter 45: Viraka guards the cave; Parvati and divine feminine allies defend the threshold.
- Chapter 46: Shiva returns and subdues Andhaka. Graphic battle details are condensed.
- Chapters 47–48: the wider battle and Shukra episode are acknowledged but not retold.
- Chapter 49: meditation, release, apology, recognition of Parvati as mother and acceptance among Shiva’s Ganas.

## Editorial care

The source distinguishes Andhaka’s bodily blindness at birth from the pride and desire that later distort his judgment after he has received sight. The page therefore never uses disability as a synonym for ignorance. “Ignorance” names a failure to recognise relationship, limit and dignity. The battle is non-graphic, and transformation is presented as acknowledgment, relinquished enmity and a changed relationship—not as avoidance of consequence.
