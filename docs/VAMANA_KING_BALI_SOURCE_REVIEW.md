# Vamana and King Bali source review

Primary source: owner-supplied *Srimad Bhagavat Mahapuran*, Gita Press Sanskrit-Hindi first volume, Skandha 8, chapters 15-23.

Scope: Bali's restoration and conquest; Aditi's payo-vrata and Vishnu's promise; Vamana's appearance and arrival at the sacrifice; the request for three steps; Shukracharya's warning and Bali's decision; the Trivikrama revelation; Bali's binding and offer of his head; the gift of Sutala, divine protection and Indra's restoration.

Editorial controls:

- Preserve the chapter sequence rather than opening only at the three-step request.
- Keep Aditi's vow concise and do not turn the page into a ritual manual.
- Present Bali as the text does here: powerful, generous, steadfast and capable of restraining his followers.
- Distinguish Vamana's brahmachari form from the expanded cosmic Trivikrama form.
- Condense combat and coercive detail for a general reader.
- Do not silently merge Onam traditions or accounts from other Puranas into this Bhagavata retelling.
- Do not reproduce long passages from the Hindi explanation or distribute the supplied scan.
