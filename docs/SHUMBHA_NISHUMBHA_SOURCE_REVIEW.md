# Devi and Shumbha–Nishumbha source review

## Supplied editions

- `Markandeya puran.pdf`: Gita Press, Gorakhpur, code 539, concise illustrated Hindi Markandeya Purana. The Devi Mahatmya arc used here is identified in its contents and printed pages 217–245.
- `Markandeya Purana.pdf`: F. E. Pargiter’s English translation of the Markandeya Purana (1904). Cantos 85–91, printed pages 488–518, provide the parallel English account.

The supplied PDFs remain private and are not copied into `src`, `dist`, or GitHub.

## Editorial boundaries

- The page follows Devi Mahatmya chapters 5–11: Kaushiki, the messenger, Dhumralochana, Chanda and Munda, Chamunda, the divine energies, Raktabija, Nishumbha, Shumbha, and the concluding praise.
- The account is paraphrased rather than quoted. Long battle catalogues and graphic details are condensed.
- The Goddess’s names and manifestations are presented in the sequence supplied by the text. Later interpretations are not silently imported.
- The reflection about changing method in the Raktabija episode is clearly labelled editorial.
- All generated artwork is labelled as interpretation and does not reproduce book illustrations.
