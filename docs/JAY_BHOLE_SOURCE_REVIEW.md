# Jay Bhole Group Ahmedabad

The introduction, founding year, address, contact details and activity context on this page follow the group's public website: [jaybholegroup.com](https://www.jaybholegroup.com/). The group began in 2006 with devotees of Shri Neelkanth Mahadev.

The works and photographs were supplied by the owner for publication: Sand Sri Yantra, Chamar, Panch Dhatu Ghanta, Paduka, Ajay Baan, and Akhand Bharat. The ceremony photograph identifies Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Vidhushekhara Bharati Mahaswamiji as the approver of the Sri Yantra design, according to the owner's confirmation.

The four YouTube links were supplied as official group videos and are linked directly from the page. The black-and-white and red Sri Yantra images were supplied as reference images and are not used as portfolio works. The Ambaji location description remains limited to the supplied Ajay Baan context; no additional temple identity is inferred.
