# Festival expansion source review — 25 October 2026

Expanded the existing three-feature page into a 31-entry directory: the three existing entries plus 28 source-verified additions. The existing illustrated dossiers remain intact below the directory. Added search, kind filters, URL restoration, live result counts and empty-state recovery; all entries remain readable without JavaScript.

New claims use only the owner's supplied Skanda Part 5/6/7, Linga Part I and Brahmanda Part I English scans. Book contents and relevant pages were rendered and visually inspected. Exact per-entry chapter/verse and printed/PDF references live in src/data/festivals.json and render centrally on Sources; source links are derived from stable entry identifiers. No PDF or private review image is published.

Skanda Part 5 supplies a Purushottama/Jagannath temple-cycle account, Part 6 the Karttika sequence, and Part 7 the Vaishakha/Ayodhya passages. Akshaya Tritiya combines two explicitly identified contexts. Tulasi Vivaha's inspected chapter uses Navami; no universal modern Ekadashi date is imposed. Vaikuntha Chaturdashi remains distinct from Vaikuntha Ekadashi. Kartika Purnima is described as completion of the Karttika vow rather than evidence for all modern Dev Diwali celebrations. The Rama-birthplace entry only claims a Navami observance because the inspected passage does not specify month/fortnight.

Recurring Shiva, Uma-Maheshvara and ancestral observances are labeled accordingly. Chaturmasya is a season, not a single-day festival. No new promise-of-results, severe-fasting instructions, modern archaeological identification, fixed Gregorian dates or unverified popular aliases are added. The broad directory does not claim to exhaust every Hindu festival or every regional practice.

The shared logo is reduced from 80px header/144px footer to 48px in both desktop placements, and from 64px header/144px footer to 40px in both mobile placements. The complete square artwork is preserved with object-fit:contain; responsive header height is reduced to match. No raster artwork was edited.
