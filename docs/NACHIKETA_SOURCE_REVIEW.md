# Nachiketa and Yama source review

Primary source: owner-supplied `Katha Upanishad - Gita Press Gorakhpur-Reduced.pdf`, 182-page scan, Gita Press code 578, Sanskrit with Shankara-bhashya and Hindi explanation.

The narrative reader uses Katha Upanishad 1.1–2.3. PDF page numbers are 12 pages greater than the printed pagination. Key inspected groups are recorded in `src/_includes/nachiketa-yama-references.njk`.

Editorial boundaries:

- Original condensed English prose, not translation.
- Atman/Self is not equated with the unchanged everyday personality.
- The supplied Shankara commentary informs the reading; alternative Vedanta interpretations are acknowledged rather than merged.
- Yama is presented as Dharmaraja and teacher, not a horror figure.
- The images are contemporary devotional and conceptual interpretations, not evidence of historical appearance.
