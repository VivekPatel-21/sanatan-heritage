# Prahlada and Narasimha source review

Primary source: owner-supplied *Srimad Bhagavat Mahapuran*, Gita Press Sanskrit-Hindi first volume, Skandha 7, chapters 3-10.

Scope: Hiranyakashipu's boon and tyranny; Prahlada's qualities and instruction; the father's repeated attempts against him; Prahlada's teaching to the children and recollection of Narada; the pillar challenge; Narasimha's manifestation and non-graphic defeat of Hiranyakashipu; Prahlada's prayer, refusal of worldly reward, request for his father and installation as ruler.

Editorial controls:

- Do not merge the popular Holika episode into this Bhagavata chapter sequence.
- Preserve the text's sequence: boon, domination, conflict over devotion, failed attempts, child teaching, pillar manifestation, prayer and compassionate request.
- Keep the manifestation and defeat non-graphic for a general reader.
- Distinguish Bhagavata theology from the editorial reflection.
- Treat twilight, threshold, lap, claws and the human-lion form as narrative responses to the boon, without reducing the story to a puzzle.
- Do not reproduce long passages from the Hindi explanation or private scan.
