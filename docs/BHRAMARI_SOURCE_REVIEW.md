# Bhramari Devi and Aruna — source and artwork review

**Owner-supplied source:** *Srimad Devi Bhagavata Purana*, Book 10, Chapter 13, translated into English by Swami Vijnananda. The main episode begins at verses 33–41 and continues to 127, PDF pages 1632–1638. Verses 1–32 provide earlier context on the Manus.

**Approach:** Original English retelling, not copied from the supplied translation. The account preserves Narada's question to Narayana; male Daitya Aruna's austerities and specified boon from Brahma; the conquest of the Devas; the voice directing worship of the Goddess; Brihaspati's intervention involving Gayatri; the appearance of Bhramari Devi surrounded by dark bees; the swarm overcoming the Daityas; and the Devas receiving blessings of devotion. Other Devi traditions and ritual instructions are not conflated with this one.
