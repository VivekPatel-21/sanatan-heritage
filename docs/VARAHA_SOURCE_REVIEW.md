# Varaha source review

Primary source: owner-supplied Gita Press Sanskrit–Hindi Bhagavata Purana, Volume 1, locally retained at .qa/creation-sources/bhagavata-volume1.pdf with searchable OCR text. No alternate translation or web summary was substituted.

Scope: 3.13.6–45 (Manu, Brahma, manifestation, rescue and praise); 3.17.16–31 (Hiranyaksha and Varuna); 3.18.1–28 (challenge, Earth secured, mace duel, Brahma); 3.19.1–38 (weapons, illusions, defeat, return, narrative conclusion).

Controls: preserve chapter 13's summary / later expanded narration distinction; Earth secured before expanded duel (3.18.8); do not invent Hiranyaksha pushing Earth underwater; distinguish Varaha from Narasimha; no blending with other Puranic versions. 3.19.25 locates the concluding blow at the ear; no graphic injuries reproduced. 3.19.29 supplies the brief attendants/cursed-birth background; chapters 15–16 are not fully retold. Sacred cosmology is not mapped to scientific geography. Prayers and combat are condensed in original English. Closing reflection explicitly editorial.
