## Editorial boundaries

- The reader is an original condensed English retelling, not a translation.
- The repeated teaching remains within the full sequence of examples.
- The supplied edition’s Shankara commentary is identified as an Advaita reading; other Vedanta interpretations are acknowledged without being merged into the retelling.
- The fast is described only as part of the textual episode and is not presented as health or practice advice.
- The cosmological sequence is not presented as modern science.
- Sat, Atman and Brahman are not personified in the illustrations.
- The closing reflection is editorial.
