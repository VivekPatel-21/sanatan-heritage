# Queen Madalasa — story source and artwork review

**Source:** Owner-supplied F. E. Pargiter English translation of the Markandeya Purana (1904), Cantos XXIV–XXIX. PDF pp. 184–201. Printed pagination differs. The narrative draws principally on XXV.1–18, XXVI.1–39, XXVII.1–31, XXVIII.1–36 and XXIX.1–46, with XXIV.42–43 as context for the homecoming.

**Editorial choices:** An original English narrative synthesis, not a copy of the English translation or a fabricated Sanskrit recital. Madalasa’s three elder sons receive instruction in detachment; the youngest, Alarka, is educated for rule after the king requests a path of worldly duty. Historic social and ritual prescriptions are attributed to their original textual setting, not universalized. No invented later events are supplied as a tidy ending. The famous modern Madalasa lullaby formula has not been inserted without confirmation from this edition.

**Art placements:** `madalasa-card.png` is the single Stories card image; `madalasa-atman.png` is the sole reader hero; `madalasa-sons.png` and `madalasa-alarka.png` are the two in-story illustrations. Four unique 1200 × 900 original cinematic PNG masters; the existing Sharp pipeline derives optimized WebP delivery formats. The scenes are symbolic artistic interpretations, not document photography or verified historical portraits.
