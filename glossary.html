WORDS & THEIR WORLDS

Glossary

Sanskrit words encountered in the stories, with Devanagari and approximate pronunciation.

dharmakarmaātmanbrahmanmokṣabhaktiśaktidevadevītapasyantrapurāṇa

धर्म

dharma

Say: DHAR-ma

Duty, ethical order and the way of living that sustains life; its meaning depends on context.

कर्म

karma

Say: KAR-ma

Action, and the consequences of action in teachings about moral cause and effect.

आत्मन्

Atman · ātman

Say: AAT-man

The Self; schools differ in their account of its relationship to ultimate reality.

ब्रह्मन्

brahman

Say: BRAH-man

Ultimate reality in Upanishadic and Vedantic thought. Brahman is distinct from Brahma, the creator deity.

मोक्ष

Moksha · mokṣa

Say: MOKE-sha

Liberation from samsara, the cycle of rebirth; its nature is understood differently across schools.

भक्ति

bhakti

Say: BHAK-ti

Devotion and loving dedication to the Divine.

शक्ति

Shakti · śakti

Say: SHAK-ti

Power or energy; divine power, especially the Goddess in Shakta traditions.

देव

deva

Say: DAY-va

A divine being or god; the feminine form is devi.

देवी

Devi · devī

Say: DAY-vee

Goddess; also a title for the Divine Mother.

तपस्

tapas

Say: TA-pas

Spiritual discipline and austerity, associated with inward effort and transformation.

यन्त्र

yantra

Say: YAN-tra

A diagram or device; in worship, a geometric form associated with a deity and contemplation.

पुराण

Purana · purāṇa

Say: pu-RAA-na

A genre of sacred narrative including divine stories, cosmology, genealogies and teachings.
