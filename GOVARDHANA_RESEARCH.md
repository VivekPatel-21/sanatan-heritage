## Decisions

Keep the Krishna-centred devotional setting explicit. Do not turn the initial debate into a general prohibition of ritual. The reader says “one hand,” as in 10.25.19; left-hand detail appears separately in the edition’s purport. No unsupported little-finger claim is inserted. The modern reflection about responsibility is clearly editorial.

New imagery is interpretive. The cover depicts a peaceful imagined pasture, not a documented ancient site. The interior recalls Indra’s supplication with Surabhi; it is not a literal reconstruction of 10.27.2. A duplicate crown in the first interior candidate was removed with a targeted Imagegen edit. Final assets were visually inspected for child Krishna’s two arms/two legs, pastoral attributes and the subjects’ anatomy. No specialist iconography certification is claimed.

The original collection lifting image retains its single placement. The new images have one visible placement each, with derivative sizes generated by the build. Prompts and paths are recorded in `IMAGE_PROMPTS.json` and `ARTWORK_LEDGER.json`.
