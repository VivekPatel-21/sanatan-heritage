# Devi and Mahishasura · research and editorial notes

Checked 11 October 2026 against Devi Mahatmya chapters 2–4. Primary translation: F. E. Pargiter, 1904 Markandeya Purana, cantos 82–84, transcribed at Wisdom Library. Sanskrit/English cross-check for chapters 2–3: Devi Mahatmya Digital Temple. Detailed chapter links are in src/_includes/mahishasura-references.njk and rendered within Sources. Verse divisions differ between presentations; the reader uses chapter references. Extended battles, prayers and gift lists are condensed into original prose without long translation excerpts.

## Sequence and scope

- Chapter 2 / Canto 82: displaced gods, united radiance, manifestation, gifts, lion and battle with the armies.
- Chapter 3 / Canto 83: Cikshura and Chamara, buffalo attack, noose and transformations (lion, armed man, elephant, buffalo), final spear and sword encounter, celebration.
- Chapter 4 / Canto 84: hymn, compassion and strength, protection in all directions, requested boon and disappearance.

The reader retains the narrative's divine conflict, without merging in Raktabija, Shumbha–Nishumbha, the Lalita account or separate Mahishasura traditions. It does not reduce all the characters to a single psychological symbol. The names Chandika, Ambika and Durga are contextualized. Combat is condensed without graphic detail; spear, sword and the emerging asura remain in the climax. The concluding reflection is explicitly editorial. Praise and requested blessings are presented as part of the scripture's devotional account.
