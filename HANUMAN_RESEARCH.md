## Verification record

- Sundara Kanda 1: ocean crossing; Mainaka, Surasa and Simhika. Cached Sanskrit/translation page and 5.1.8 were accessible. The Surasa encounter was explicitly verified as a divine test, not a lethal encounter.
- Sundara Kanda 2: small form and arrival in Lanka; indexed Sanskrit/translation text accessible.
- 5.13.2 and surrounding indexed commentary: failed palace search and decision to search the Ashoka grove.
- Sundara Kanda 17: recognition of Sita, concealment in the shimshupa tree; indexed Sanskrit/translation text accessible.
- Sundara Kanda 31: story of Rama spoken within Sita's hearing; full Sanskrit/translation page accessible.
- 5.36.1–2: messenger identification and Rama's inscribed ring; indexed Sanskrit/translation text accessible.
- Sundara Kanda 37: small/large form and Sita's reasoning, including 5.37.32 and 44; indexed primary text and commentary accessible. No invented dialogue added.
- Sundara Kanda 48: Indrajit's Brahmastra and Hanuman's decision to tolerate capture, 5.48.43–48; indexed Sanskrit/translation text accessible.
- Sundara Kanda 53: Vibhishana's intervention, punishment and Sita's prayer, 5.53.1–3 and 24–31; indexed Sanskrit/translation text accessible.
- 5.54.12–20: burning, explicitly sparing Vibhishana's house; indexed primary text/translation accessible.
- Sundara Kanda 55: Hanuman's alarm, anger self-reproach and renewed knowledge that Sita is unharmed; indexed Sanskrit with Govindaraja commentary accessible.
- 5.56.1–9 and 34: farewell and return leap; indexed primary text/translation and commentary accessible.
- Sundara Kanda 58: university summary of report to companions accessible. Battle sequence intentionally condensed.
- Sundara Kanda 65: full page accessible, especially 5.65.7 (salutation toward Sita), 21–24 (chudamani and message) and 27 (token/report).

Direct IIT page requests frequently timed out or returned 502. Search-indexed primary passages were used where noted. No claim of complete line-by-line review of every intervening sarga. Do not advertise an exact critical-edition verse alignment: the references follow the portal's numbering. The reader's reflection is explicitly labelled editorial. The closing uncertainty remains faithful to the narrative: Sita is found but not yet rescued.
